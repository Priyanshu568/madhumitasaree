Our Story

A Ranaghat name, forty-six years running

Madhumita Fab Fashion began in 1980 on Subhas Avenue with a simple promise: honest fabric, fair prices and sarees that look as good in the wardrobe as they do on the shelf. Today we bring together everyday cotton tants, fine jamdanis and wedding silks, so one visit can dress the whole family for the season.
